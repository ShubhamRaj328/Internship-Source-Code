Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

To reset your password:

  1. Click on "Forgot password?" on the login page
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Check your email for reset instructions
  4. Follow the link in the email to create a new password

How do I add new products to my inventory?

To add new products:

  1. Navigate to the Products section in the dashboard
  2. Click the "Add Product" button
  3. Fill in the product details (name, description, price, etc.)
  4. Upload product images
  5. Set inventory levels and save

How can I track my sales performance?

You can track sales performance through:

  • The main dashboard overview with key metrics
  • The Analytics section for detailed reports
  • Sales reports filtered by date range
  • Product performance analytics
  • Customer behavior insights

How do I manage customer orders?

Order management features include:

  • View all orders in the Orders section
  • Update order status (pending, processing, shipped, delivered)
  • Print shipping labels and invoices
  • Send order updates to customers
  • Handle returns and refunds

What browsers are supported?

Clothes4U Admin supports the following browsers:

  • Google Chrome (recommended)
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari (macOS)
  • Microsoft Edge

For the best experience, please keep your browser updated to the latest version.
